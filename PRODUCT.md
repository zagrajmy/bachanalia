# Product

Who this is for and what it is trying to do. How it is built is
[`IN_PROGRESS.md`](./IN_PROGRESS.md).

## Users

Polish fantasy fandom — RPG players, board gamers, cosplayers, SF&F readers —
mostly from Lubuskie and western Poland, and from the whole country in 2026,
because the edition holds Polcon rank. They arrive from a Facebook post or a
friend's message, usually on a phone, deciding whether to buy accreditation
and what to expect. Secondarily: programme contributors, exhibitors, partners.

## Purpose

The official site of Bachanalia Fantastyczne, run by ZKF Ad Astra in Zielona
Góra since the 1980s. The 40th edition ("XL") is 25–27 September 2026. The
site has to say date, place and price instantly, sell accreditation, and show
the programme, the guests and the partners. Editors work in WordPress; this
renders it.

## Personality

Crafted, communal, veteran. Made by fans for fans for forty editions: warm and
playful, with the confidence of an institution. Not corporate, not a template.

## Anti-references

- AI-purple gradient "epic fantasy" pages, parchment-and-dragon kitsch.
- Corporate event templates: speaker-card grids, countdown timers, stock crowd
  photos.
- The old site's generic Ashe/Elementor look.

## Principles

- Date, place and the ticket CTA readable in three seconds.
- **A printed ticket, not a landing page.** White paper, navy ink, coral
  accent, dashed rules, prices set like a tariff.
- Real content over filler: real prices from WooCommerce, real partner names,
  real guest history. A placeholder says it is one.
- Poster energy, web discipline: big type and committed colour, but WCAG AA,
  reduced motion honoured, and static rendering.
- Write the fact or write nothing. No line that only restates the heading
  above it.

## Accessibility

WCAG AA contrast, verified per colour pair rather than assumed. Polish
content (`lang="pl"`) and latin-ext faces, because the diacritics are not
optional. `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured everywhere. Every navigation
group is a real destination, reachable by keyboard, never a dead toggle.
